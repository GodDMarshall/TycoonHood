# 3D campus asset credits

Every file the optional 3D campus at `/world` loads is a Tycoonhood original.

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `tex/*.jpg` | Generated by `scripts/world/generate-textures.mjs` | Tycoonhood original |

and geometry are built in code, and its labels are the app's own text.
