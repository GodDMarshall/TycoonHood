The Divine Maestro of Compassion & Wisdom

His Holiness Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Swamiji

Sri Swamiji is a revered spiritual leader and the head of Avadhoota Datta Peetham in Mysuru, renowned for His profound spiritual teachings, music therapy and dedication to humanitarian service.

He was born on 26 May 1942 at Mekedatu — the sacred confluence of the Arkavati, Kaveri and Guptagamini rivers — to Sri Narasimha Shastry (later Sri Narahari Teertha Swami) and Sri Jayalakshmi Maata. The child was born on a rock in the Kaveri, covered with holy ash, while His mother was in deep meditation. From childhood He served those who came to Him, understanding their past, present and future and helping them overcome their difficulties.

The Spiritual Successor

Sri Datta Vijayananda Teertha Swamiji

Fondly called Sri Bala Swamiji, He is the junior pontiff of Avadhoota Datta Peetham. Born in Mysuru on 14 January 1977 and educated at the SGS Veda Pathashala, He was anointed by Sri Swamiji in 2004 and given the Sannyasa name Sri Datta Vijayananda Teertha.

Revered for His wisdom, humility and gift for rendering the Vedas in simple language, Sri Bala Swamiji observes the Chaturmasya Deeksha every year from Guru Purnima, when His discourses kindle spiritual passion in thousands of listeners.

Guru Parampara

“Gu” is darkness and “Ru” is the light that dispels it; “Parampara” means uninterrupted succession. The Guru Parampara of Avadhoota Datta Peetham traces its origin to Lord Dattatreya, the Adi Guru — the embodiment of the Holy Trinity, Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva — through whom divine wisdom and blessings flow from generation to generation.

A spiritual science for every belief

Datta Kriya Yoga

A blend of ancient wisdom and modern technique for physical, mental and spiritual well-being. Sri Swamiji selects specific mental, pranic and physical practices to address modern-lifestyle imbalances — renewing the Kriya Yoga principle that unites Hatha Yoga and Raja Yoga.

Taught worldwide for decades to health, educational and business communities, Datta Kriya Yoga transcends religious boundaries and deepens mind-body coordination and self-awareness.

Serving every being

Compassion at the Peetham reaches cows, children, patients and birds alike.

Gomaata — Mother Cow

Kamadhenu, Kalpavriksha, Amruta and Mother Lakshmi emerged together in creation as the most splendorous embodiments of divinity. The scriptures declare that all Devatas reside in the cow, so Sanatana Dharma reveres her as Gomaata.

Sri Swamiji has established a large Goshala at the Mysuru Ashram that shelters and cares for more than 50 cows, with Goshalas maintained at other branches too.

SGS Hospital — serving with compassion

Since 1978, Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Hospital has run a comprehensive healthcare system within the Holistic Health Centre, providing preventive and curative care for outpatients and inpatients. Sri Swamiji has always emphasised primary health care over costly tertiary care.

1,512cases at the 2024 free medical camps

31dental procedures

Annual free medical camps are held during Sri Bala Swamiji’s Chaturmasya Vrata, including cancer screening for women over 40. A hospital blood-donation drive with local organisations collected over 200 units for emergency services.
