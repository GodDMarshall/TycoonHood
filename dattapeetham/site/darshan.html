    Learn · Significance

    Datta Peetham Mandir Nagari

    At the heart of the Peetham is Lord Dattatreya, the presiding deity who embodies the Sadguru — the Master Teacher principle. The Mysuru Ashram is home to six shrines and a pathway of 366 Birth Date Pillars.

    Lord Dattatreya guides seekers from the shadows of the material world to the illumination of the inner soul.

    As you enter

    1. 01

      Hanumadwaram

      A magnificently sculpted welcome arch with Hanuman idols.

    2. 02

      Infant Dattatreya

      An intricate carving of the infant form of Lord Dattatreya.

    3. 03

      Vedic chants

      Young boys chant the Vedas under the attentive guidance of their teacher.

    4. 04

      The shrines

      Congregational prayer, the Mother Goddess, Lord Venkateshwara and Hanuman.

    01 Datta Temple · Sachchidanandeshwara · Panchayatana Devasthana

    Universal Prayer Hall

    As its name implies, the Universal Prayer Hall is an auditorium where daily congregational prayers take place, drawing a large number of devotees who gather in the divine presence of Sri Swamiji. It houses the main deity of the Peetham — Kaalaagni Shamana Dattatreya Swami.

    The most revered worship, the Sri Chakra Puja, is performed at the Sri Chakram Meru, enshrined in a special Mantap to the right side. To truly appreciate the sanctity of this Puja, one must witness it in person.

    Deities of the hall

    • Kaalaagni Shamana Dattatreya
    • Sri Chakram Meru
    • Ganapathy
    • Narasimha Swami
    • Shiva
    • Surya Narayana
    • Vishalakshi Devi
    • Sachchidanandeshwara

    On the right of the hall, the Panchayatana Devasthana houses five small temples; on the left, a Shivalaya is dedicated to Lord Shiva as Sachchidanandeshwara.

    Divine descent
    9 June 1978 — Lord Datta as Sri Kaalaagni Shamana Dattatreya
    Re-consecrated
    Rama Navami, 21 April 2002, at the centre of the hall
    Kshetra
    The first of the sixteen Shodasha Datta Kshetras
    Jaya Guru Datta

    Datta worship, the Datta Stava and the greeting Jaya Guru Deva Datta were introduced in that week of June 1978; by the mid-eighties the greeting had become Jaya Guru Datta.

    The Datta temple opens daily at 6 AM. Tailabhisheka every Sunday; every evening, Divya Naama Sankeertana and Kakada Arati.

    02 Mother Goddess · 28 December 2023

    Rajarajeshwari Temple

    On the left side of the Universal Prayer Hall is the most beautifully and intricately designed temple for Mother Goddess Rajarajeshwari, consecrated by Sri Swamiji on 28 December 2023 during Meena Lagna, at 12 noon.

    As devotees circumambulate the deity they see the intricately designed forms of the Nava Durgas, the miniature forms of the deities of the 18 Shakti Peethams, and several Devi Yantras and Mandalams. Sri Swamiji has also installed a big Sri Chakra, to which devotees can personally offer prayers with Kumkum Archana.

    03 Lord Hari · 28 December 2023

    Hari Sannidhi

    2,108rare Salagramas, in the form of Ananthanaga Shayana

    The most spectacular Hari Sannidhi stands to the immediate right of the Rajarajeshwari temple, unveiled by Sri Swamiji on 28 December 2023.

    The Trivikrama Salagrama and the Manoratha Kurma Salagrama, weighing 300 kg, have also been installed inside the Sannidhi. A specially made Sudarshana Yantra on top of the Sannidhi was designed and conceptualised by Sri Swamiji.

    04 Sri Datta Venkateswara Kshetram · 1999

    Sri Datta Venkateshwara Temple

    For those seeking spirituality and peace, a visit to Sri Datta Venkateswara Kshetram is a must. Pilgrims say that this temple for Lord Venkateswara pulsates with Divine Energy. Its architecture is much like the one in holy Tirumala, and it is the firm belief of Datta devotees that the Lord of the seven hills has Himself come to Datta Peetham.

    Before the temple stands a huge Dhwaja Stambha — the sacred flag post — made from a 150-year-old teak tree, brought from the deep forests of Kerala. Several thousands throng the temple on Vaikuntha Ekadashi every year, and Annadanam is conducted for the needy on every Purnima.

    05 The tallest stone statue of Hanuman in India

    Karya Siddhi Hanuman Temple

    Sri Swamiji consecrated the magnificent 70-ft Karya Siddhi Hanuman statue in 2012, in the precincts of the Sri Datta Venkateshwara Temple Complex. This huge monolithic statue is the tallest stone statue of Lord Hanuman in India.

    Poorna Phala Deeksha

    Tie a coconut, pray for fulfilment

    Devotees worship Hanuman by tying a whole dry coconut for wish fulfilment — a sixteen-day vrata.

    1. 01

      Collect the Poornaphala

      Visit the Pooja counter to collect the Poornaphala, then proceed to the Hanuman temple.

    2. 02

      Sankalpa

      The priests lead the Sankalpa before the coconut is tied; then circumambulate the temple sixteen times.

    3. 03

      Pradakshinas

      On any four days of the sixteen, perform 16 Pradakshinas each time.

    4. 04

      Mantra & discipline

      Chant the Karyasiddhi Anjaneya mantra 108 times daily; refrain from non-vegetarian food, alcohol and smoking.

    5. 05

      Prasadam

      On the 16th day, untie the coconut and make a sweet dish of it, taken as Prasadam.

    06 Ashta Bhairava · 14 December 2016

    Kalabhairava Temple

    The Ashta Bhairava temple was consecrated by His Holiness Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Swamiji on 14 December 2016. Eight Bhairava idols were consecrated as per Shaiva Agama rites, in the south-east corner of the Ashrama.

    When the Ashrama started in 1966, an underground tunnel existed at this place, leading to the Chamundi hill; it was closed in the mid-80s. Today this subterranean temple has one main shrine for the Pradhana Bhairava Murthy, with seven small niches around it holding the other seven Bhairava Murtis.

    In the shrine

    • Pradhana Bhairava
    • Seven Bhairava Murtis

    07 Along the pathway

    Birth Date Pillars

    366pillars, on either side of the pathway

    366 pillars are installed on either side of the pathway in the Ashrama. They are called the “Date of Birth Pillars” — each pillar has a specific deity and demigod for that particular day.

    Festivals

    Festivals through the year

    Maha Shivaratri, Sri Swamiji’s Birthday, Navaratri and Datta Jayanti are the most auspicious events at the Ashram — a blend of Vedic rituals, classical music, dance and drama. Their dates follow the Hindu calendar.

    Coming up at Mysuru

      Vaikuntha EkadashiAt the Sri Datta Venkateswara Temple, Mysuru — with Gita Jayanti at Nada Mantapa.
    • 21–23 Dec 2026
      Dattatreya JayantiKsheerabhisheka & Punya Smarana, Panchamrita Abhisheka, and Sahasra Kalasha Thailabhisheka to Dattatreya Swami on Datta Jayanti Purnima.
    Official calendar
    December · three days

    Datta Jayanti

    The incarnation day of Adi Guru Dattatreya: Abhisheka in the Universal Prayer Hall, bhajans and a Shastra Sadas — and the one day in the year devotees may offer oil ablutions to the Moola virat.

    Sep or Oct · nine days

    Navaratri

    Sri Swamiji honours Mother Goddess Raja Rajeshwari through the Nava Avarana Puja, with Kumari Puja, Sri Chakra Puja, Devi Homa and concerts at Nada Mantapa.

    A night of vigil

    Maha Shivaratri

    Sri Swamiji performs Rudra Abhishekam to the Sachchidanandeshwara Shiva Lingam, followed by Sahasra Kalasha Abhishekam by devotees; musicians offer Sangeeta Seva until late night as devotees keep vigil, and at 3 AM Sri Swamiji gives His discourse on Shiva.

    Late May

    Sri Swamiji’s Birthday

    Celebrated around 26 May, with exact dates set by the Hindu calendar. Sri Bala Swamiji performs Pratyaksha Paada Pooja to Sri Swamiji; mobility aids are given to the physically challenged and Annadaana is offered on a large scale.
