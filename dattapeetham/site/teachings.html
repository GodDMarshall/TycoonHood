    Literary contributions

    A library of wisdom

    168 titles published by Avadhoota Datta Peetham, in Kannada, Telugu, English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati and Sanskrit.

    Bhaktimala magazine

    Devotion, every month

    Bhaktimala, “a Garland of Devotion”, is the Peetham’s monthly magazine. It began in 1976 to spread spiritual awareness and moral values, especially among students.

    • Seven languagesIncluding Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Hindi, English and German.
    • Now in the appAll Kannada and English monthly issues since October 2022 are in the Bhaktimala app.

    Sanatana Dharma Parirakshana

    Protecting the eternal way

    Inspired by Lord Dattatreya’s teachings, Sri Swamiji has toured India extensively to promote Sanatana Dharma, and welcomes everyone, of any faith or none.

    Propagation of the Bhagavad Gita

    Through Gita Mahayajna, envisioned by Sri Swamiji in 2015, students memorise all 700 verses. On 20 July 2024, 8,000 people recited the entire Gita at NOW Arena, Chicago.

    SGS Gita (India) SGS Gita Foundation (USA)

    SGS Veda Pathashala

    Classes in all four Vedas and in Sanskrit, in the traditional Gurukula system, for around 120 students at the Mysuru Ashram.

    Veda Nidhi

    Homas & Yaagas

    Ganapathy Homa wherever Sri Swamiji is; Pavamana Homa at the Mysuru Ashram on every full moon day; the Ati Rudra Yaaga at Srisailam in 2023.

    Gau Samrakshana

    The scriptures declare that all Devatas reside in a cow, reverentially called Gomata. The Gau Shala at the Mysuru Ashram houses more than 50 cows.

    Establishment of temples

    Over 80 branches across India, among them 16 Ashrams for the 16 incarnations of Lord Dattatreya, Datta Kasi, Datta Rameshwaram and 9 Navanatha Kshetras.

    Wisdom in practice
