      Before you write
      
      Questions visitors often ask
      
      
      Many answers are already in the Peetham’s guidelines for visitors. If yours is not here, call or write to the Ashram office.
      
      
      
        Write to the Ashram
        Call
      
      
      
      Answers are drawn from the Peetham’s Guidelines for Visitors, its Contact page and its homepage.
      
    
      
    
      
  What are the temple darshan timings?
  
      The Peetham’s homepage gives the Temple Timings as 6 AM – 12 PM in the morning and 4 PM – 8 PM in the evening, and lists Abhisheka at 7 AM and Aarti at 6 PM daily at the Hanuman temple.
      Some shrines keep their own hours: the Karya Siddhi Hanuman and Sri Datta Venkateshwara temple pages list different weekday and weekend timings. Please check the temple you plan to visit.
      All temple timings 
      

      
  Can I stay at the Ashram?
  
      Persons of all faiths are welcome, but facilities are limited and are primarily maintained for devotees of Sri Swamiji. Visitors are requested to stay in hotels in Mysuru and visit the Ashram daily for its programmes.
      Those seeking a brief stay should first attend Ashram programmes consistently for at least a week; the Ashram Administrator handles such inquiries. No visitors may stay while Sri Swamiji or Sri Bala Swamiji are out of station, and during major functions all newcomers must find accommodation elsewhere.
      Hotels near the Ashram 
      

      
  Whom do I ask for permission to visit and stay?
  
      Even those known to the Ashram staff and to Sri Swamiji should ask in advance, by letter, phone, fax or email, to H. V. Prasad, personal secretary to Sri Swamiji. Once your plans are final, notify the Ashram of your arrival date, the number in your party and your expected duration of stay.
      Carry photocopies of a Government-approved identity proof or passport, and passport photos, for every member of your family or group; they are submitted to the Ashrama office on arrival, as a government regulation.
      

      
  How do I reach the Ashram?
  
      The Ashram is at Datta Nagar on Ooty Road (Nanjangud Road), Mysuru. For a first visit from overseas the Peetham recommends flying to Bengaluru and hiring a taxi to Mysuru from the desks in the airport arrival hall.
      In Mysuru, city bus 119 runs from the City Bus Stand directly to the Ashram, and 119A goes via JSS Medical College. Auto rickshaws and cabs from the railway station take about 15–20 minutes.
      How to reach, step by step 
      

      
  What if I arrive late at night?
  
      The Ashram’s gates close at 11 PM, so if you arrive late at night, please stay in a hotel. Travelling by road from Bengaluru to Mysuru after 9 PM is not advised.
      

      
  Where do I report a problem with the website or the apps?
  
      For all website application and programme issues, please use Feedback / Support. This page, and the Ashram office, are for personal requests and messages.
      

      
  Can I book a puja or make a donation here?
  
      No. Puja bookings and donations are made on the Peetham’s existing online system, not through this form: book a puja or make a donation there.
