    The festival year

    Twelve months, one sacred cycle

    The Ashram’s festivals follow the Hindu lunar calendar — the Panchanga — so they move against the Gregorian calendar from year to year. Choose a month to see what is celebrated.

    Chaitra

    about Mar to Apr

    Yugadi

    Chaitra Shuddha Pratipada — the lunar new year

    2026Yugadi programmes at SGS Ashrama, Dundigal, Hyderabad, 16–20 March; Yugadi fell on 19 March.

    Sri Rama Navami

    Chaitra Shuddha Navami

    2026Sri Ramanavami Festival at the Dundigal Ashrama, Hyderabad, 27 March.

    Vaishakha

    about Apr to May

    Sri Jayalakshmi Maata Aradhana

    Vaishaakha Panchami

    Every year Sri Swamiji conducts special rituals and worship on the Aradhana day of His mother, Sri Jayalakshmi Maata.

    2026The 2026 calendar also lists a Jayalakshmi Mata Aradhana Festival on 1 February.

    Sri Jayalakshmi Maata

    Jyeshtha

    about May to Jun

    Sri Swamiji’s Birthday

    Born 26 May 1942, Jyeshtha Shuddha Ekadashi

    Thousands of devotees gather every year. Sri Bala Swamiji performs Pratyaksha Paada Pooja to Sri Swamiji; wheelchairs, crutches, walkers and other support equipment are given to the physically challenged, and Annadaana is offered on a large scale.

    202684th Birthday celebrations, 24–31 May, with the Pratyaksha Padapuja on 27 May (Adhika Jyeshtha Ekadashi).

    Ashadha

    about Jun to Jul

    Guru Purnima

    Ashadha Purnima, also called Vyasa Purnima

    The Guru Parampara is specially worshipped. After the Guru Parampara Pooja and Vyasa Pooja, Sri Bala Swamiji begins His Chaturmasya Vrata Deeksha.

    2026America Panchaashat Nadee Jala Samarpana for Sri Swamiji, Dallas, 29 July.

    The Guru Parampara Pooja

    Chaturmasya Vrata Deeksha

    From Guru Purnima, for four fortnights or four months

    During the Chaturmasya Sri Bala Swamiji leads Yagnas and Homas, discourses on the Puranas and holy texts, extensive Annadana and free medical camps.

    1,512cases at the 2024 free medical camps

    2025In 2025 — the 22nd Chaturmasya — at Sripada Vallabha Anagha Datta Kshetram, Pithapuram, 10 July to 7 September.

    Shravana

    about Jul to Aug

    Krishna Janmashtami

    Shravana Bahula Ashtami

    2026Krishna Janmashtami Festival at the Mysuru Ashrama, 4 September.

    Sri Narahari Teertha Swami Aradhana

    In the lunar month of Shravana

    Every year Sri Swamiji conducts the Aradhana of His father, Sri Narahari Teertha Swami (Narasimha Sastry).

    2026Narahari Teertha Aradhana on 20 August at the Hanuman Temple, Datta Yoga Center, Erin, Canada.

    Varamahalakshmi Vrata

    A Friday in Shravana

    2026Observed on 21 August at the Hanuman Temple, Datta Yoga Center, Erin, Canada, during Sri Swamiji’s visit.

    Bhadrapada

    about Aug to Sep

    Vinayaka Chaturthi

    Bhadrapada Shuddha Chaturthi, after Swarna Gowri Vratam

    2026Ganapathy Festival at SGS Ashrama, Vijayawada: Swarna Gowri Vratam on 13 September at 6 PM; on 14 September, Ksheerabhisheka to Kshipra Ganapathy Swami, Sahasra Modaka Ganapathy Homa and Varasiddhi Vinayaka Vratam.

    Chaturmasya concludes

    Bhadrapada Purnima

    A Vijaya Yatra with all the disciples and devotees who kept the vrata. Before crossing a water body, everyone gathers to chant the 11th chapter of the Bhagavad Gita, Vishwarupa Sandarshana Yoga, and Gita books are given away.

    Ashwayuja

    about Sep to Oct

    Devi Navaratri

    Nine days from Ashwayuja Shuddha Pratipada — September or October

    Sri Swamiji honours Mother Raja Rajeshwari with the Nava Avarana Puja. Each day begins around 8 AM with a procession from the Datta Temple to Nada Mantapa, then Kumari Puja, Sri Chakra Puja, Devi Homa and concerts, ending around 8:30 PM with Devi bhajans sung by Sri Swamiji.

    2026Devi Navaratri Festival at Datta Peetham, Mysuru, 11–20 October.

    Kartika

    about Oct to Nov

    The Chaturmasya’s traditional close

    Ashadha to Kartika

    Taken as four months, the Chaturmasya vow runs from Ashadha (around July) to Kartika (around November). The Vedas also allow each month to be represented by a fortnight.

    Margashirsha

    about Nov to Dec

    Vaikuntha Ekadashi

    Shuddha Ekadashi in Dhanurmasa — December or January

    Several thousands of local people throng the Sri Datta Venkateshwara temple to take a glimpse of Lord Venkata Ramana Swamy on this most auspicious day.

    202620 December at the Sri Datta Venkateswara Temple, Mysuru — with Gita Jayanti at Nada Mantapa.

    Temples & darshan

    Datta Jayanti

    Margashirsha Purnima · three days in December

    The incarnation day of Adi Guru Dattatreya: Abhisheka to the Dattatreya idol in the Universal Prayer Hall, memorial services, bhajans and a Shastra Sadas for scholars — and the one day in the year devotees may offer oil ablutions to the Moola virat.

    202621–23 December at Datta Peetham, Mysuru, ending with Sahasra Kalasha Thailabhisheka on Datta Jayanti Purnima.

    Pausha

    about Dec to Jan

    New Year Darshana

    1 January — a fixed date

    2026New Year Darshana at SGS Ashrama, Vijayawada.

    Magha

    about Jan to Feb

    Ratha Saptami

    Magha Shuddha Saptami

    2026Ratha Saptami at SGS Ashrama, Kurnool, 25 January.

    Maha Shivaratri

    Magha Bahula Chaturdashi · a night of vigil

    On the eve, the Venkatamakhin Memorial and Tyagaraja Aradhana with mass singing of the Pancharatna kritis. On the night itself, Rudra Abhishekam to Sri Sachchidanandeshwara by Sri Swamiji, Sahasra Kalasha Abhishekam by devotees and Sangeeta Seva until late; at 3 AM Sri Swamiji’s discourse on Shiva, and Maha Mangala Arati around 6 AM.

    2026Maha Shivaratri Festival at SGS Ashrama, Mysuru, 15 February.

    Regular programmes

    The rhythm of Ashram life

    Between the great festivals the Mysuru Ashram keeps a steady rhythm of worship, music and teaching — through the day, the week and the year.

    Every day

    From the Sri Chakra Puja to evening bhajans

    1. 01

      Sri Chakra Puja

      Around 8 AM, performed by Sri Swamiji or Sri Bala Swamiji when in town. Devotees may also sponsor rituals such as Homa and Tailabhishekam.

    2. 02

      Teertha

      Traditional holy water is offered after the Sri Chakra Puja.

    3. 03

      Datta Kriya Yoga

      Devotees gathered in the Universal Prayer Hall practise Pranayama or a Mudra.

    4. 04

      Evening bhajans

      Divya Naama Sankeertana every evening for at least one and a half hours, led by Sri Swamiji and elder members of the Ashram.

    Daily discourse

    Yoga Vasistham

    Sri Bala Swamiji gives daily lectures on the Yoga Vasistham, in Kannada and Telugu.

    Sundays

    Darshana & bhajans

    Sri Swamiji gives darshana on Sunday mornings when in town, and large numbers of devotees come from great distances to sing with Him.

    Every month

    Narasimha Kalyana

    The celestial wedding of Lord Lakshmi Narasimha, performed every month on Swati Nakshatra.

    Participate
    Through the year

    Camps & seminars

    Yoga camps, music concerts for meditation and healing, debates, seminars and sports events for all age groups.

    Seva camps · Chaturmasya

    Discourses, Annadana and free medical camps

    Every year from Guru Purnima, Sri Bala Swamiji observes the Chaturmasya Vrata Deeksha, offering its fruits for universal peace and welfare. Through the period He gives discourses and leads Annadana and free medical camps — including cancer screening for women over 40.
