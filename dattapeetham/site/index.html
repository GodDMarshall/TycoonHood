    Peethadhipathi · Avadhoota Datta Peetham

    A Life of Divine Compassion

    His Holiness Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Swamiji is a revered spiritual leader and the head of Avadhoota Datta Peetham in Mysuru, renowned for His profound spiritual teachings, music therapy and dedication to humanitarian service.

    His mission is carried forward by Sri Datta Vijayananda Teertha Swamiji, fondly called Sri Bala Swamiji, anointed Junior Pontiff by Sri Swamiji on 29 May 2004, who explains the deep spiritual essence of the Vedas, Shastras, Upanishads and Puranas in simple words.

    Virtual tour

    Immersive Visual Experience

    Walk through the Mysuru Ashram in six stops, from the Universal Prayer Hall to Nada Mantapa. Keep scrolling to move from one to the next.

    01 / 06 · Darshan

    Universal Prayer Hall

    Sri Swamiji calls it the crown of the Ashram. Daily congregational prayers gather here before Kaalaagni Shamana Dattatreya Swami, the main deity of the Peetham.

    The Prayer Hall
    02 / 06 · Darshan

    Rajarajeshwari Temple

    Consecrated by Sri Swamiji on 28 December 2023. Devotees circumambulate the Mother Goddess past the Nava Durgas and the miniature forms of the 18 Shakti Peethams.

    The temple
    03 / 06 · Darshan

    Karya Siddhi Hanuman

    The 70-foot monolithic statue consecrated in 2012 is the tallest stone statue of Hanuman in India. On weekends, 3D projection mapping is synchronised with the Hanuman Chalisa.

    The temple
    04 / 06 · Attraction

    Shuka Vana

    Founded by Sri Swamiji in 2012: a free-flight, walk-in aviary and bird hospital, home to more than 2,000 birds of 486 species. It holds a Guinness World Record (6 May 2017) for the most bird species in an aviary; the certificate records 468. (Its official page currently notes that it is temporarily closed to the public for maintenance.)

    Shuka Vana Watch: Varam the parrot
    05 / 06 · Attraction

    Bonsai Garden

    More than 450 carefully shaped miniaturised trees in an oriental garden setting: the Kishkindha Moolika Bonsai Garden, passionately developed and nurtured by Sri Swamiji Himself.

    The garden
    06 / 06 · Music

    Nada Mantapa

    The Hall of Musical Tones, inaugurated on 31 May 1998: a 35,000 square foot auditorium with state-of-the-art sound, lighting and theatre systems.

    Nada Mantapa Watch: Its construction

    Nada Chikitsa

    Healing through music

    Dr. Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Swamiji is a renowned composer, singer and musician. He understands the ancient music system of Raga-Ragini and has created many new ragas using it. Because His compositions are so prolific, the title Sagara, “ocean”, was given to express this form of Raga-Ragini, and His concerts of music for meditation and healing carry the name Raga Sagara.

    A spiritual science for every belief

    Datta Kriya Yoga

    A blend of ancient wisdom and modern technique for physical, mental and spiritual well-being. Sri Swamiji selects specific mental, pranic and physical practices to address the imbalances of modern life.

    Taught worldwide for decades to health, educational and business communities, Datta Kriya Yoga transcends religious boundaries.
