# Ojasphera Labs — logo

## The idea

**Ojas** (Sanskrit): vitality, radiance, inner energy. **Sphere**: the system.

The mark is an eclipse at its "diamond ring" moment: a rim of light wrapping a
dark core, and the one brilliant point where the light breaks through.

- **The core** is the real-world thing: the business, the project, the problem.
- **The rim** is what Ojasphera builds around it: the intelligence. It is heaviest
  where it meets the spark, the way light gathers at an eclipse's edge.
- **The spark** is *ojas*, the moment a system comes alive.

It is also an **O**, and it is the same object the homepage renders in 3D.

## Construction

The mark is drawn on a 100×100 canvas:

- **Rim:** an outer circle R44, minus an inner circle r32 offset 5.2 away from
  the spark.
- **Spark:** a circle r7.4, 40.2 from centre at −46°, with a 3.3 clearance cut
  into the rim.
- **Wordmark:** Geist Semibold, all caps, tracked +18%, converted to outlines,
  so it never depends on an installed font.
- **Source:** the geometry is generated by `scripts/brand/mark-geometry.mjs`
  (exact circle intersections, no masks). The output is also in `geometry.json`.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep the spark's diameter (≈15% of the mark) clear on every side.
- **Minimum size:** mark 16 px (use the favicon tile below 24 px); horizontal lockup 96 px wide.
- **The spark** is always amber or the same single colour as the rim. Never another hue.
- **Don't** rotate, stretch, outline or add effects to the mark. Don't move the
  spark, and don't place the white mark on light backgrounds.
